## Workspace and keys

An API key belongs to **one workspace** and sees all of it. Keys are created and revoked by workspace admins in **Settings → API** (10 active keys at most); every admin is notified when one is created. Keys are stored only as a fingerprint: Agentova can't show a key again after creation.

A workspace on the free plan has API access. Access is closed (`403 workspace_access_denied`) only while the subscription is unpaid, paused or incomplete.

## Leads

<LeadSources />

`GET /leads` can filter on `source` and `agent_id`. The `agent_id` of a phone lead is the phone agent, exposed under the same `agt_…` prefix as the others.

## Events

<Events />

Webhook subscriptions filter by **event type** only: a subscriber receives every event of that type in the workspace. Filter on `source`, `agent_id`, `status`… on your side.

## Runs

A run is one execution of an automation, with the list of actions it took and their result. `status` is `success` when every action succeeded, `failed` when every action failed, `partial` otherwise (for example an email reply that couldn't be sent and was kept as a draft).

<RunsByChannel />

Runs are kept for **90 days**, then deleted.

## What v1 doesn't cover

- Creating, editing or deleting automations; triggering one manually.
- Conversations, contacts, phone calls, meetings, publications, documents.
- Leads from social conversations and CRM sources.
- Per-key permissions: every key sees the whole workspace.
