A lead is created — and **Lead created** fires — from three places:

| Source | Where the lead comes from |
|---|---|
| `widget` | The contact form of your website chatbot, when a visitor fills it in |
| `lead_ads` | A Facebook or Instagram Lead Ads form, handled by a Lead Ads automation |
| `telephony` | A phone call qualified by your phone agent |

These **don't create leads**: private messages, comments and emails your agents reply to, and CRM sources (HubSpot, Typeform, Close…). The API accepts `social` and `crm` as source filters, but no lead carries them yet.
