| Type | What it does | Channels (`provider`) |
|---|---|---|
| `social_comments` | Replies to comments on your posts | Facebook, Instagram |
| `social_story` | Replies to story replies | Instagram |
| `social_messages` | Replies to private messages | Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp |
| `lead_ads` | Handles Lead Ads forms (message to the prospect, add to your CRM) | Facebook, Instagram |
| `email_messages` | Replies to emails | Gmail, Outlook, IMAP |
| `crm_source` | Acts when a contact or a response arrives in a CRM or a form tool | HubSpot, Close, Monday, ClickUp, Typeform, Tally, Odoo, Axonaut… |

**Not covered in v1:** phone agents, the website chatbot itself (it's an agent, not an automation), scheduled social posts, and builder workflows. An automation that was deleted or turned off in the Agentova app, or whose account is disconnected, doesn't appear at all.
