Each recipe lists the steps to create in the Zap editor. Replace the example names with yours.

## Pause an automation at night, reactivate it in the morning

Useful when your team wants to answer messages itself outside office hours — or the opposite.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Zap 1 — Trigger: Schedule by Zapier">
    **Every Day**, at 7:00 pm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action: Agentova — Pause Automation">
    **Automation**: pick your automation in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Zap 2 — Trigger: Schedule by Zapier">
    **Every Day**, at 8:00 am.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action: Agentova — Activate Automation">
    **Automation**: the same automation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Running the morning Zap on an automation that's already active is harmless: nothing changes and no error is raised.

<Warning>
  Nothing that arrives during the night is caught up in the morning. For an **email** automation, Agentova stops listening to the mailbox while it's paused: the emails received during the night **never get a reply** from your agent, even after the morning reactivation. Pause an email automation at night only if your team answers those emails itself.
</Warning>

## Get alerted when an automation stops working

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger: Agentova — Automation Status Changed" />
  <Step title="Filter by Zapier">
    Only continue if **Status** exactly matches `error`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action: Slack — Send Channel Message (or Gmail, SMS…)">
    Message: “The automation *Name* (*Agent Name*) stopped on an error — check its connection in Agentova.” — insert the **Name** and **Agent Name** fields from the trigger.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This catches the failures: revoked access to an account, a mailbox that refuses the sign-in, a channel that can't be reconnected. When a failure takes an automation away, this last `error` is the only signal: when it arrives, the automation has already left the lists, until its account is reconnected. Deleting an automation or turning it off in the Agentova app sends nothing.

## Get alerted when a reply fails

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger: Agentova — Run Completed" />
  <Step title="Filter by Zapier">
    Only continue if **Status** is `failed` (or `failed` **or** `partial` to also catch half-failures).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action: your alert channel">
    Include the Automation ID, the Trigger Type and Occurred At, so the team can find the conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send every new lead to a spreadsheet or your CRM

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger: Agentova — New Lead" />
  <Step title="(Optional) Filter by Zapier">
    Keep only one source, for example **Source** exactly matches `lead_ads`, or one agent with **Agent ID**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action: Google Sheets — Create Spreadsheet Row (or HubSpot, Pipedrive…)">
    Map Name, Email, Phone, Source and Created At to your columns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Log everything your agents do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger: Agentova — Run Completed" />
  <Step title="Action: Google Sheets — Create Spreadsheet Row">
    One row per run: Occurred At, Automation ID, Trigger Type, Status. Handy for a weekly count of what your agents handled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pause an automation during your holidays

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger: Google Calendar — Event Start">
    Search for events containing “Holidays”.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action: Agentova — Pause Automation" />
  <Step title="Second Zap: Google Calendar — Event Ended, then Agentova — Activate Automation" />
</Steps>

The same warning as for the night pause applies: what arrives during your holidays isn't caught up when you come back.
